Privacy Policy — Smashwave

Effective October 24, 2026 · updated October 25, 2026 (music setting)

This policy explains what data the Smashwave game for Android handles, why, and the choices you have. Smashwave is developed by Patrick Allan, an individual developer based in Brazil. Contact: prkallan1337@gmail.com.

Summary

  • You play without an account, login or sign-up. We run no servers of our own and receive no personal data from you.
  • Your settings (sound, vibration, quality, etc.) and your records stay on your device only.
  • The game shows Google AdMob ads only when you choose to watch one to get a second chance. Google's SDK collects some device data to serve ads, measure results and prevent fraud.
  • Where required by law (such as in the European Union and the United Kingdom), the game asks for your consent before data is used for personalized ads.
  • We do not sell personal data.

1. Data stored on your device

The game saves your settings locally: music and sound effects on or off, vibration, visual quality, reduced effects, frame-rate limit and aim inversion. This data never leaves your device, does not identify you, and is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear the app's data.

The game also stores your records locally: best time and eliminations in Free mode and, in the Campaign, the levels you won with the best remaining base health. They are not sent to any server and are deleted together with the app's data.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Smashwave uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob), provided by Google LLC. Ads are rewarded and optional: they appear only when, after losing a match, you tap Second chance (shown as Segunda chance) to watch a video. There are no ads during combat.

To work, Google's SDK automatically collects and shares with Google, according to Google's own documentation:

  • IP address, used to estimate the device's general (approximate) location;
  • App interactions, such as app launches, taps and video views;
  • Diagnostic information, such as launch time, hang rate and energy usage;
  • Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and the app set ID.

This data is used to serve ads, measure their performance (analytics) and prevent fraud, and is encrypted in transit (TLS). The game may also use Android Privacy Sandbox features (topics and attribution) when Google's SDK relies on them.

Google's processing is governed by Google's policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • How Google uses advertising

3. Consent and your choices

  • Consent: the game uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). In regions where the law requires it, such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a form asks for your choice before data is used for personalized ads. Without consent, ads may be non-personalized or limited.
  • Changing your choice: in those regions, Options → Ad privacy (shown in Portuguese as Privacidade dos anúncios) reopens the form at any time.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads (the path may vary by manufacturer) and manage personalization at Google My Ad Center.
  • Not watching: watching ads is always optional. If you never tap Second chance, no ad is shown, although the SDK may start and preload an ad in advance.

4. Android permissions

  • Internet and network state: needed to load ads.
  • Vibration: for haptic feedback on Smash and boss defeat; can be turned off in Options.
  • Advertising ID and Privacy Sandbox (AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_*): used by Google's ads SDK.
  • Keeping the screen awake and technical services (WAKE_LOCK, FOREGROUND_SERVICE): declared by Google's libraries for their internal operation.

The game does not access precise location (GPS), camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files, and has no in-app purchases, chat or social features.

5. Children

Smashwave is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has provided data through the game, please contact us so we can help.

6. Legal bases and your rights

As the developer, we do not receive or store personal data on servers. Processing for advertising is carried out by Google; where the law requires it, it depends on your consent, collected through the form described above.

Under Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law 13,709/2018) and equivalent laws such as the GDPR, you may request confirmation of processing, access, correction, anonymization, portability, deletion, information about sharing, and withdrawal of consent. Since we do not keep your data, we can explain how to exercise these rights with Google, which controls the data collected by the ads SDK. Contact us at the email below. You may also contact Brazil's data protection authority (ANPD) or the authority in your country.

7. Retention and security

Local settings remain on the device until the game is uninstalled or its data is cleared. Data collected by Google's SDK is retained according to Google's policies. All SDK communication is encrypted in transit.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes, for example when new features are added. The date at the top shows the current version; relevant changes will be announced on this page.
